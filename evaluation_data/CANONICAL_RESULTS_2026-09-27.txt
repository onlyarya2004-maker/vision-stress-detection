==============================================================================
READING THESE NUMBERS
==============================================================================
* Frame-level accuracy is the familiar figure but its n is inflated:
  frames within one recording are highly autocorrelated.
* Session-level accuracy uses the honest denominator (recordings),
  which is why its confidence interval is much wider.
* Default-mode live recordings now span 4 distinct subjects (see the PER-SUBJECT
  breakdown above) - this is real cross-subject evidence, not within-subject only.
  Studying/Hospital/Driving modes still have no live recordings yet, so those remain
  single-subject or empty until Phase 1's team recordings extend to those modes.
