  frames within one recording are highly autocorrelated.
* Session-level accuracy uses the honest denominator (recordings),
  which is why its confidence interval is much wider.
* All live recordings to date are SINGLE-SUBJECT. These measure
  within-subject behaviour, not cross-user generalisation.
